PROJECT AMERICA HQ 2.2 — METHOD OPTIMIZATION

- Giant A strength methods are now movement/variant specific.
- Bench Press defaults to paused strength work; chest-supported rows to top-set + back-offs; pull-up variants to clusters; carries to density progression.
- Giant B hypertrophy methods are now movement/variant specific.
- Incline DB press defaults to straight work; machine/Smith incline press can use Myo-Reps.
- Pulldown / assisted pull-up prefer Rest-Pause. RDL remains straight sets only.
- Rear delts prefer Myo-Reps; biceps DC Rest-Pause; triceps Myo-Reps.
- Widowmaker is rotated rather than prescribed every week.
- Giant B now automatically rotates a 1–2 movement serious-intensifier budget across sessions unless the user saved a custom default.
- Lateral raises default to Myo-Reps; FST-7 remains available as an occasional specialization option.
- The workout card now shows OPTIMAL MATCH and explains when a compatible intensifier is intentionally rotated off today.
- Existing user-saved method defaults remain authoritative.
- Offline cache and backup metadata updated to 2.2.
