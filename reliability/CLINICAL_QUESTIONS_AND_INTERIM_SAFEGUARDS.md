# Knee and elbow — questions for a qualified clinician, and conservative interim safeguards

**This is not medical advice and it does not prescribe rehabilitation, return-to-running, jumping or high-intensity knee loading.** It lists what the app currently does that touches those areas, the questions that need a professional answer, and ways to *do less* inside the existing app until you have answers. No code was changed for this section.

## 1. App behaviour that touches the knee or elbow (VERIFIED in `audit/PROJECT_AMERICA_HQ_2_3_AUDIT.md`)

- Loaded stretch holds follow every exercise slot. The quad, hack, leg-press, leg-extension and Bulgarian split squat holds share the same text: "deep pain-free knee-flexed position under control".
- Optional `bl` (Giant B Lower): 11 direct quad sets (17 counting intensifier efforts) in one session, with a leg-extension FST-7 (7 stages) and a Widowmaker.
- Giant B cycle 2 includes a single Widowmaker leg-press set of 15–20 reps.
- DC Rest-Pause is the default on biceps curls, and Myo-Reps on triceps pressdowns, in Giant B.
- Incline-curl, Bayesian-curl and overhead-triceps loaded stretches are long-length elbow positions.
- Thursday athletic day: 10 m ×3, 15–20 m ×2, CMJ 3×3, pogos, skater→stick.
- The app records no side (left/right), no pain, no rest or tempo. Readiness now has a joint override (Fix 3), but it is a self-report scale only.

## 2. Questions for your orthopaedic surgeon, sports physician or physiotherapist

**Knee (left ACL / MCL / medial meniscus history)**
1. What is my current status for each structure (graft type, repair or meniscectomy, date, any residual laxity or effusion)? Are there activity or range-of-motion limits I should treat as hard?
2. Which of these are acceptable now, and at what loading, if any: leg press, hack squat, leg extension, Bulgarian split squat, RDL, leg curl? Is deep flexion under load acceptable, and to what depth?
3. Are quadriceps and hamstring strength symmetry tests (limb symmetry index) available to me? What numbers should I meet, and how often should they be retested? (Reinjury risk has been linked to quadriceps asymmetry in the literature, but the numbers are yours to get from a clinician.)
4. What weekly set limits, and what limits for a single session, do you recommend for quadriceps work? Is a rapid increase in quad volume acceptable?
5. Are high-rep, high-fatigue knee efforts (Widowmaker, FST-7 leg extension) acceptable, or should they be avoided?
6. What must be true before any running beyond the current 10–20 m accelerations? What must be true before jumping (CMJ, pogos, skater landings), and before sprinting at maximal speed? Which tests or criteria do you use?
7. Should hop and landing-mechanics testing be done before the athletic day continues as written?
8. What symptoms should make me stop a session, and which should make me contact you (swelling, giving way, locking, pain pattern, next-day soreness)?

**Elbow (bilateral tendinopathy history)**
9. Which elbows and tendons are involved (lateral, medial, distal biceps, triceps)? What is the current irritability level?
10. Are long-length loaded holds (incline or Bayesian curl, overhead triceps) acceptable? Is rest-pause or high-rep failure work on curls and pressdowns acceptable?
11. What pain level (scale and timing, during and next-day) is the acceptable ceiling during loading?
12. Are pulling and gripping volumes (weighted pull-ups, rows, carries) a problem for the elbows? Do you want a limit on weekly weighted pull-up sets?

**General**
13. What is the recommended order of reintroducing changes, one variable at a time?
14. Do you want me to log pain or side-to-side data? If so, in what format (0–10, location, timing)?

## 3. Conservative interim safeguards (restrict-only; use existing app controls; clinician can override)

These reduce exposure. None of them add exercises, add volume or prescribe progression. They are offered as options for you to adopt, not as medical instructions.

1. **Stop on pain.** Any joint pain beyond what your clinician has allowed, or any swelling, giving way or locking, ends that exercise. Log it in the session notes.
2. **Use the readiness check-in honestly.** Fix 3 now stops a low Joint status from showing GREEN. A 1/5 shows RED and a 2/5 shows YELLOW at best.
3. **Hold off on the optional `bl` day** until the quad-volume questions are answered. The normal A+B week does not include it.
4. **Choose "Straight Sets" for knee and elbow-dominant exercises** (leg extension, leg press, hack squat, curls, pressdowns) using the intensifier selector already in the app, until your clinician agrees otherwise.
5. **Treat loaded stretch holds as optional.** Skipping them on knee and elbow-relevant slots removes the deep-flexion and long-length loading without changing any program.
6. **Keep to the written athletic-day distances.** Do not lengthen sprints or add jumps, landings or loaded plyometrics until you have clinical criteria (question 6).
7. **Use COMPACT mode on poor days**, which strips intensifiers and caps sets.
8. **Change one thing at a time**, and keep a written note of what changed.
